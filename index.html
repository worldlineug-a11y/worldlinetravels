About World Line Travels

One trusted point of contact for regional travel.

World Line Travels Uganda is a Kampala-based travel and transport agency providing practical travel solutions across East Africa. Our services cover bus bookings, flights, accommodation, local transport, forex and travel-support needs.

Whether you are travelling for business, family, tourism or a regional connection, our role is to help organize the moving parts of your journey and connect you with the right travel service.
